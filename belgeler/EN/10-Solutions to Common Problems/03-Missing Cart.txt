# Missing Cart
Sometimes DAs comes to us with problems and their most common problem is a missing cart. Sometimes some DAs accidentally took someone else's cart without even realizing. They scan the correct cart, but took another cart which usually stands near to their actual cart; when they distracted by something. When a DA comes to you and says that his cart is not in the designated staging location, first you must check the neighbouring staging locations. How? By scanning one package from them with PS-Overview and checking it's status. If the status is "In Transit" you can take a note of this route's number and DSP's name and you can contact with this DSP and show your notes and ask them to check this route. Once you detect the mistakenly taken carts and informed the DSPs about it, DSPs have to deal with the rest and do the cart changing.
