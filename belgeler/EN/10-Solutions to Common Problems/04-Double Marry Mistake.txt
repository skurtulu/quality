# DOUBLE MARRY MISTAKE
[GÖRSEL: doublemarry.png, 500x300, orta]
Sometimes pickers marry 2 different bags in one stow location. Although system usually only sees the last married bags in that location, sometimes we may see 2 open bags in one location on the stow system. To prevent confusions; we can follow this steps to scrub stow location;
TC -> Stow -> Manage stow area -> Click on the question mark at the right corner on the screen -> Scrub location 
After you scrubbed the location, you can marry your desired bag to the stow location.
