# Accidentally Selecting My Cart is Full Option
When pickers accidentally choose my cart is full option when their cart is not full yet; they may not know how to cancel this selection. It's actually pretty simple; they can choose the "Cancel (Stornieren)" option on their TC and they can continue to collect their items.

If they stage their cart after they accidentally selected "my cart is full" option on their TC; there is no going back from that. They have to take a new cart.
