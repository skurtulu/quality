# INCORRECT CART SCAN
[GÖRSEL: wrongcart1.jpg, 300x300, orta]
Sometimes pickers scan a cart at the beginning of their tour but mistakenly take another empty cart; or, after getting a picklist, they go to the restroom and scan a cart, but take another cart with them when they return to collect. In these cases, you can click the question mark in the upper right corner of the screen and select "cart not found".
[GÖRSEL: exclamation.jpg, 30x30, sol]
It's important to be sure that pickers didn't start to collect their items, you can only mark a cart as missing when you are sure that it should be empty.
