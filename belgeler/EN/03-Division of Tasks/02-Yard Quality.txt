# YARD QUALITY
[GÖRSEL: yardqual.jpg, 300x200, orta]
Yard Quality is the person who handles issues related to packages coming from Yard, handles repack matters, oversees staging locations, and gives feedback to supervisors about the departure process.

*Yard Quality Tasks 🎯

✅ Repack, handling damage
✅ Handling HAZMAT
✅ Cube out & Driver rejected
✅ Reporting disposed&B2FC parcels on Quip
✅ Checking not departed routes after each wave
✅ Checking and reporting wrongly parked carts left by DAs on Quip
✅ Refilling cardboard racks at the repack station
