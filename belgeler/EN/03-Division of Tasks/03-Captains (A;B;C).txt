# CAPTAINS (A;B;C)
[GÖRSEL: captain.jpg, 300x200, orta]
Captains are quality team members responsible for performing shopfloor quality tasks that fall outside the scope of POC and Yard Quality's responsibilities.

*Captain Tasks

✅ Finding packages that cancelled by customers before and during Pick & Stage process     
✅ Quick fix with tape gun
✅ Handling double stages
✅ Handling pick missing
✅ Finding and retraining people who makes mistakes during any process (except yard processes)
✅ Leftovers check
✅ Creating new location QR codes for missing ones
✅ Performance list
✅ Playing music
✅ Finding carts with mistakenly stacked items, giving feedback to the responsible people and ensuring that they learned the correct way
✅ Reporting damage bags
✅ Cleaning spillages during pick and securing the effected area
✅ Ensuring all 3 weekly Dragonfly reports are completed on time.
