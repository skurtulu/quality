# Supporting Pick & Stage
Picking is quite a tiring process. Pickers start to get tired, especially towards the final waves. At their most exhausted moments, we can support them by walking around the aisles and delivering items to them. By entering the aisle, following the lights on the bag racks, and when the light turns on, pulling the bags from the rack and sliding them towards the pickers without damaging them, we can ensure they are delivered to their carts. This way, they only need to scan the bags and load them onto their carts.
We can also collect oversized boxes by following the lights and gently sliding them across the floor to the pickers in the same way. With this help, we both make the pickers' job easier and shorten the picklist time by 1.5-2 minutes.
