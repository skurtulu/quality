# PLANNING OFF-WORK ACTIVITIES
[GÖRSEL: piknik.jpg, 400x400, orta]
To improve the work environment, we can plan off-work activities that everyone can participate in and contribute to voluntarily. Such activities soften our prejudices towards different cultures, which increases cooperation and solidarity in the workplace and prevents conflicts. I planned a picnic where everyone brought food from their own culture, and everyone loved it. Now we have a cultural food party that I organize every year.
